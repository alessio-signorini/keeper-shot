- Single ES-module pipeline worker; MediaPipe/OpenCV loaders call `importScripts`, which module workers lack, so `worker/cv.ts` shims it with sync XHR + global eval.
- OpenCV.js, MediaPipe wasm and libheif are copied to `dist/` by vite-plugin-static-copy (flattened) and precached by the service worker for offline use.
- `estimateAffinePartial2D` and `seamlessClone` are absent from the OpenCV.js build, so the similarity fallback is a small JS RANSAC and Laplacian-pyramid blending is always used.
- Source patches are warped with `cv.warpAffine` (Lanczos) on just the source region the ROI needs; masks, Lab colour transfer and pyramid blending also use OpenCV.
- Source frames are decoded in full (not cropped regions) because `createImageBitmap` crop + EXIF orientation behaves inconsistently across browsers; the preview uses a small LRU cache of downscaled frames.
- Preview uses an incremental `Compositor`: a pick change restores only the affected ROIs from the base and re-blends them plus any overlapping picks (closure), in stable person order.
- `/#debug` shows landmarks, boxes + person IDs, swap masks, sampled ORB inlier matches (stored per frame), alignment inliers/toRef and the score table.
- Faces not assigned to a person (dropped passers-by) are removed from the session.
- Frames without `DateTimeOriginal` keep file order after dated frames.
- The amber "low confidence" badge uses `best total < LOW_CONFIDENCE_TOTAL` (config).
- EXIF export copies the allow-listed tags from the base JPEG via piexifjs, falling back to the parsed EXIF (Make/Model/DateTimeOriginal) for non-JPEG bases such as HEIC.
- Detection scans the whole frame plus overlapping 640px and 400px tiles (`TILE_SIZES`): MediaPipe's detector shrinks its input to ~128px, so whole-frame detection misses group-photo faces. Faces cut by an interior tile edge are discarded; duplicates merge on IoU or centre-in-box.
- The face model is closed after the detect pass so OpenCV loads with less memory pressure; upload thumbnails are decoded one at a time at 160px, and EXIF is read sequentially.
- Every tile candidate is re-run through the landmarker on a full-resolution crop (`REFINE_*`): this rejects hands/props and gives landmarks, blendshapes, sharpness and thumbnails from full-res pixels instead of ~70px faces. Printed faces (e.g. a photo cutout) are still detected as people.
- Blinks are flagged when eyes < `BLINK_THRESHOLD` or < `BLINK_REL` × the person's most-open eyes in the burst, since baseline openness varies by person and smile.
- Selection is best-face-first: each person's highest-scoring face is the goal; the base is the frame holding the most of those best faces (within `WIN_EPSILON`), then highest summed score, then best alignment; everyone else is swapped to their best feasible face when it beats the base face by `SWAP_GAIN_MIN` (0.02). Fewer swaps is only a tie-break.
- Save uses the share sheet only on phones/tablets (where it offers Photos/Files); on desktop it downloads the file. A separate ⬇ button always downloads.
- Review shows a coloured rectangle around each face (white = base face, blue = eyes swapped, purple = face swapped, amber = check) with a small status chip, so faces stay visible.
- Playwright launches Chromium with `--disable-dev-shm-usage --disable-gpu` (container /dev/shm is tiny); the 20MP `family` fixture needs ~1GB free for the browser.
- Alignment: after background (ORB) alignment, each frame is also aligned using the faces themselves (`core/faceAlign.ts`: face-pair RANSAC seeds, then a similarity fit on eye/nose/mouth landmarks of matched faces). Whichever transform lines up more faces with the reference frame becomes `toRef` (`Frame.alignMethod`). Needed when the background aligns differently from the group (parallax, grass); on the kids burst faces that line up went from 90 to 130 of 178 and fragmented "people" from 39 to 28. A similarity (not affine/homography) fit keeps single-row groups stable.
- A person not detected in the base frame defaults to `skip` (paste nothing): they are usually still there but turned away, so pasting a face created doubled "ghost" faces. The face sheet offers "None · leave photo as is" for them; manual face picks are still possible. They are amber only if seen in at least half the frames. (BUILD.md allowed auto `face` placement here.)
- Known limitations: kids who move relative to the group still split into separate "people" (position-only grouping; an identity-embedding model would fix this). A hand or prop covering part of the source face is pasted along with it.
- Memory: one `FaceLandmarker` instance switches options between scan and refine (a second instance cost ~100MB of WASM heap); the pipeline worker is terminated after analysis (WASM heaps never shrink) and previews decode straight to the target size with `createImageBitmap` resize. Export frees the preview caches first. Needed because 20MP bursts crashed the renderer in ~2GB containers.
- Fixture status (people found / expected): big-family 48/52, family 15/13, kids 29/19, portrait 2/1. Over-splitting (moving subjects) and a few undetected faces remain.

- Grouping also matches faces within `GROUP_MAX_DIST` face widths, then merges fragments that never share a frame when their distance is within `GROUP_MERGE_BASE + GROUP_MERGE_PER_FRAME × frame gap` (movers). Verified by unit test only; fixture counts not re-measured.
- Cutout/printed faces count as people (family expects 14 = 13 + the birthday-girl cutout). People whose median face sharpness is < `MIN_REL_SHARPNESS` × the median person's are dropped (out-of-focus background mistaken for a face; was 15 vs ~290 typical).
- Face sheet: faces whose pose/shape make a swap infeasible are no longer disabled; tapping one forces a face swap (labelled 'tap to force'). Auto-selection still respects feasibility.
- Frames where fewer than `FRAME_MIN_ALIGNED` (0.6) of the reference frame's faces line up after alignment are left out (warning logged), as long as `FRAME_MIN_KEPT` (4) remain. kids: 26 → 23 people (expected 19; four cutouts + heads-down kids explain the rest).
- Base choice: highest achievable summed score (each person at their best feasible face) wins; sums within `BASE_SUM_TOL` × people count as equal and then prefer fewer swaps, more best faces in place, better alignment. Previously 'wins' (best faces already in place) ranked first, which could favour a base with a poor unfixable face.
- Review: ◻︎ button toggles face markers; wheel/pinch/+− zoom, and once zoomed the photo can be dragged to pan (clamped by hand in `panzoomchange` so it always covers its frame; panzoom's own `contain` mis-positioned the centred stage). The holder has no dark background.
- Face swaps measure colour-match statistics over the skin interior (face oval shrunk to 80%, minus brows/eyes/mouth, only where the source covers the ROI), falling back to the edge ring if too small; eye swaps still use the ring.
